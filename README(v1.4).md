## 🛡️ Core Architectural Pillars & Protocols

### 1. The TONE Protocol & Intent Gravity Calculus
Unstructured human language is often messy, emotional, and context-dependent. The **TONE Protocol** acts as a universal semantic bridge between humans and machine reasoning:
* **The Tone Header Envelope:** Every interaction is wrapped in an immutable metadata envelope containing `Actor ID`, `Intent`, `Context`, `Density`, and `Boundary Constraints`.
* **Dynamic Operational Modes:**
  * **Light Tone (`EXPAND` Mode):** Engages during low-risk, creative, exploratory states to connect ideas and support cognitive flow.
  * **Dense Tone (`SUPPORT` Mode):** Automatically triggers during high-stakes, stressful, or boundary-sensitive moments to stabilize, protect, and isolate the user.
* **Intent Gravity Calculus:** System autonomy is mathematically governed by the gravity of an intended action:
  $$\text{Gravity} = \text{Impact} \times \text{Irreversibility} \times \text{Scope} \times \text{Uncertainty}$$
  Low-gravity actions execute smoothly in the background; high-gravity operations automatically demand mandatory ethical review and explicit human authorization.

### 2. AURA: Epistemic Authority vs. Democratic Oversight
The architecture strictly decouples epistemic consensus from governance legitimacy:
* **AURA (Epistemic Authority):** Functions as the constitutional validator, drift mitigation engine, and multi-agent coordination hub. AURA orchestrates an "incident-hive" of Digital Twins across regional clusters, validates consensus against immutable constraints, and classifies systemic states into four structured tiers:
  * **Class I (Informational):** Emerging weak signals.
  * **Class II (Watch):** Moderate risk with elevated probability.
  * **Class III (Warning):** High risk of multi-domain impact.
  * **Class IV (Critical):** Imminent danger to life or critical infrastructure.
* **Human-in-the-Loop (Democratic Authority):** Machine intelligence advises; human governance decides. The Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) board validates the integrity of the process and democratic legitimacy, ensuring no high-stakes advisory is deployed without human certification.

### 3. Execution Trust, Stealth Containment & Honeypot Harvesting
Traditional perimeter security relies on admission trust (login validation). The Sommer Architecture enforces **continuous execution trust**:
* **Post-Handshake Behavioral Baselines (Layers 54–56):** Initial authentication establishes the boundary handshake. AURA continuously monitors behavioral primitives (latency, interaction pacing, hesitation, and command syntax) without intrusive surveillance.
* **The "Movie Pause" Quarantine:** If anomalous behavior or unverified access rerouting is detected, the system does not crash or execute a disruptive hard halt. Instead, it initiates a seamless visual "pause."
* **Sublayer Environment Exchange:** The live internet and sensitive local vaults are instantly severed, silently swapping the environment into an air-gapped, simulated sandbox. The intruder or compromised agent remains quarantined as an isolated test subject, allowing the system to harvest threat tactics and forensic payloads without exposing real assets.

### 4. Zero-Knowledge Vaults (ZKV) & Edge Sovereignty
* **"Detect Anomalies, Not People":** User data is not harvested into centralized corporate lakes. All biometrics, behavioral habit profiles, and personal memories reside locally in an encrypted, edge-computed Zero-Knowledge Vault.
* **Selective Disclosure:** Cryptographic proofs allow users to verify credentials, permissions, and attributes without exposing underlying raw data.

### 5. Layer 63: The Immutable Decision Ledger
* **Permanent, Append-Only Auditability:** Every unit of intent, model confidence score, tone header, self-veto, and HITL override is cryptographically signed and stored in an append-only ledger.
* **Recursive Integrity:** Historical records are never overwritten. Discovered errors or updated threat models append amendments through a strict loop (*Sense → Interpret → Respond → Update → Propagate*).
* **The Compliance Tattoo:** If operational drift exceeds a 6% margin from constitutional baselines, the visual compliance seal automatically revokes, alerting auditors to immediate misalignment.
