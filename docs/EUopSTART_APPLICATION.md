## Section 2: Project Abstract & Purpose of Pre-Financing

BY SOMMER is preparing a full Step 2 application for the EIC Accelerator to mature its TONE Sovereign Architecture from TRL 5/6 to TRL 8. The TONE architecture provides a privacy-preserving bio-telemetry and autonomous civic dividend platform utilizing:

1. **Edge Active Inference** — Variational free-energy divergence (𝓕Δ ≥ 3.0) computed locally on edge devices, triggering hardware circuit breakers to freeze autonomous financial transactions during physiological anomalies (Patent Claim 1).
2. **Zero-Knowledge Identity Proofs** — Pseudonymous `cpr_zk_hash` matching (salted SHA-256, GDPR Recital 26) enabling municipal compliance verification without exposing CPR numbers or health data (Patent Claim 2).
3. **Autonomous Surplus Dividend Ledger** — Daily civic efficiency accrual (Δ_surplus = 2.30 DKK/day) distributed non-custodially to citizens on December 10th each year (Patent Claim 3).

The architecture has a deployed and verified prototype: 9 Base44 database entities, 4 deployed backend functions, 4 dashboard views, and a 77-test Python integration suite validating all 15 patent claims against the mathematical and legal constraints.

**EUopSTART funding will be utilized exclusively to engage specialized Danish deep-tech proposal consultants, legal IP counsel (formal FTO assessment for DKPTO priority TONE-2026-PCT-01), and financial auditors to assemble the 6 mandatory annexes required for the 4 November 2026 EIC Step 2 cut-off.**

## Section 4: Detailed Expense Justification

### 4.1 External Proposal Consultancy — DKK 50,000

| Item | Description | Cost |
|------|-------------|------|
| EIC Accelerator grant consultant | Specialized Danish consultancy for Step 2 full proposal drafting, including Part B narrative (Excellence, Impact, Implementation), Work Package descriptions, and annex preparation | 35,000 |
| Technical writing review | Independent technical review of the 50-page proposal for clarity, completeness, and alignment with EIC evaluation criteria | 10,000 |
| Translation & formatting | Professional English-language editing and EU Funding Portal formatting compliance | 5,000 |

**Rationale:** EIC Accelerator full proposals require a 50-page Part B narrative across 3 evaluation dimensions (Excellence, Impact, Implementation). The success rate is 5-6.6% per cut-off (800-1,200 proposals, 60-75 funded). Professional grant writing significantly improves competitiveness.

### 4.2 Legal Freedom-to-Operate (FTO) — DKK 30,000

| Item | Description | Cost |
|------|-------------|------|
| FTO search report | Plougmann Vingtoft (or equivalent Danish patent attorney) conducts formal freedom-to-operate search for TONE architecture components (active inference, ZK compliance, surplus ledger) | 20,000 |
| EPO/DKPTO priority review | Review of DKPTO priority filing TONE-2026-PCT-01 for completeness, claim scope, and PCT international phase readiness | 7,000 |
| Annex 2 drafting | Formal FTO search report formatted as EIC Accelerator Annex 2 | 3,000 |

**Rationale:** EIC Accelerator Step 2 requires Annex 2 (Freedom to Operate search report) as a mandatory submission. A formal FTO from a Danish patent attorney (Plougmann Vingtoft / Zacco / Patrade) is essential for credible IP defense during the jury interview (Step 3).

### 4.3 Financial & Compliance Audit — DKK 20,000

| Item | Description | Cost |
|------|-------------|------|
| Financial capacity documentation | Certified Public Accountant prepares Annex 1 (Financial Information): P&L, cash flow, cap table, and 70% co-funding capacity verification (~€750K applicant contribution) | 12,000 |
| Bogføringsloven compliance audit | Verification that the autonomous surplus ledger (Δ_surplus = 2.30 DKK/day, Dec 10 payout) meets Danish accounting standards for 5-year retention | 5,000 |
| AI Act conformity pre-assessment | Pre-assessment of TONE architecture against EU AI Act Annex III requirements for the Annex 6 Ethics Self-Assessment | 3,000 |

**Rationale:** EIC Accelerator Step 2 requires Annex 1 (Financial Information) and Annex 6 (Ethics Self-Assessment). The 70% co-funding rate requires demonstration of ~€750K (DKK 5.6M) applicant contribution capacity. A CPA-certified financial statement is mandatory.
